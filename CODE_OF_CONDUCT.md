# Code of conduct

pstsmith follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and leave
personal attacks, harassment and discrimination out of every space of this project (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anywhere the project is represented).

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/baozdemir/pstsmith/security/advisories/new) (choose "Report a
vulnerability" and say that it is a conduct report), or by a direct message to a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are
read only by the maintainers and kept confidential. You will get an answer within 14 days.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on reports as the Contributor Covenant 3.0 describes, with the aim of repairing harm: from a
private conversation, to a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
